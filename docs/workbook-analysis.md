## Executive Summary
- **Total Workbooks**: 8 (Grades 3 to 10)

- **Total Units**: 64 (8 units per grade)
- **Total Activities**: 128 (2 activities per unit)
- **Total New Words / Glossary Terms**: 192 (24 per grade, 3 per unit)
- **Total Final Projects**: 8 guided capstones with grade-specific templates
- **Total Certificates**: 8 completion certificates with student avatar & teacher sign-off
