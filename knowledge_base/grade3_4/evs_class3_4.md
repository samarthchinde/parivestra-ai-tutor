# Class 3 and 4 Environmental Studies (EVS) Knowledge Base

## Chunk: evs_c3_plants
Title: Plants and Parts of a Plant
Subject: EVS
Topic: Plants
Grade: Class 3
Keywords: plants, roots, stem, leaves, flower, photosynthesis, sunlight, water
Content:
Plants are living things that make their own food using sunlight, air, and water. A plant has different parts that do important jobs. Roots grow underground to hold the plant firmly in the soil and drink up water and minerals. The stem stands tall like a straw to carry water up to the branches. Leaves are the green food factories of the plant. Flowers make seeds so new baby plants can grow.

## Chunk: evs_c4_animals
Title: Animals and Their Habitats
Subject: EVS
Topic: Animals
Grade: Class 4
Keywords: animals, habitat, forest, desert, ocean, polar, herbivores, carnivores
Content:
A habitat is the natural home where an animal finds food, fresh water, and shelter. Camels live in hot sandy deserts because they can store fat in their humps and survive with very little water. Polar bears have thick warm white fur to stay cozy in cold snowy Arctic ice. Fish breathe underwater using gills. Animals that eat only plants are herbivores, like cows and deer, while animals that hunt other animals are carnivores, like tigers and eagles.

## Chunk: evs_c3_water_weather
Title: Water and Seasons
Subject: EVS
Topic: Water
Grade: Class 3
Keywords: water, rain, clouds, evaporation, winter, summer, monsoon, weather
Content:
Water is essential for all living creatures, plants, and people. The sun warms water in rivers and lakes, turning it into invisible water vapor through evaporation. Up in the cool sky, the vapor forms fluffy clouds. When clouds get heavy, raindrops fall back down as rain. This endless journey is called the water cycle. In India, we experience summer, monsoon, autumn, winter, and spring seasons.

## Chunk: evs_c4_body_health
Title: Our Body and Healthy Habits
Subject: EVS
Topic: Human Body
Grade: Class 4
Keywords: body, sense organs, eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin, hygiene, exercise
Content:
Our body is an amazing machine with 5 special sense organs. Eyes help us see colorful sights, ears help us hear sounds and music, our nose smells flowers and food, our tongue tastes sweet, sour and salty flavors, and skin helps us feel soft or hot things. To keep our bodies strong, we need nutritious food like vegetables and milk, daily active play, drinking plenty of clean water, and 8 to 9 hours of peaceful sleep each night.
