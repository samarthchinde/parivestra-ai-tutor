# Class 3 and 4 Mathematics Knowledge Base

## Chunk: maths_c3_numbers
Title: Numbers and Place Value
Subject: Maths
Topic: Numbers
Grade: Class 3
Keywords: numbers, digits, place value, hundreds, thousands, ones, tens
Content:
Place value tells us how much each digit in a number is worth. In a 3-digit number like 345, the digit 5 is in the ones place, 4 is in the tens place (meaning 40), and 3 is in the hundreds place (meaning 300). When we write numbers, 10 ones make one ten, and 10 tens make one hundred. In Class 4, we also learn thousands, where 10 hundreds make one thousand.

## Chunk: maths_c3_addition
Title: Addition and Putting Groups Together
Subject: Maths
Topic: Addition
Grade: Class 3
Keywords: addition, plus, add, sum, total, carrying, regrouping
Content:
Addition means combining two or more numbers to find the total sum. For example, if you have 15 mangoes and your friend gives you 12 more, you add them together: 15 + 12 = 27 mangoes. When the sum of digits in the ones place is 10 or more, we carry over 1 ten to the tens column. Always start adding from the ones column on the right side.

## Chunk: maths_c4_multiplication
Title: Multiplication Fun and Repeated Addition
Subject: Maths
Topic: Multiplication
Grade: Class 4
Keywords: multiplication, times, multiply, product, tables, groups
Content:
Multiplication is a fast way of doing repeated addition. If there are 4 baskets and each basket holds 5 shiny apples, we can write 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 20, or simply 4 times 5 = 20. The answer in multiplication is called the product. Any number multiplied by 0 equals 0, and any number multiplied by 1 stays the same. Learning multiplication tables helps you solve math puzzles quickly!

## Chunk: maths_c4_fractions
Title: Fractions and Equal Parts
Subject: Maths
Topic: Fractions
Grade: Class 4
Keywords: fractions, half, quarter, numerator, denominator, equal parts
Content:
A fraction represents an equal part of a whole thing. If you divide a round pizza into 4 equal slices and eat 1 slice, you ate 1 out of 4 slices, written as 1/4 (one quarter). The top number is called the numerator and tells how many parts you have. The bottom number is called the denominator and tells how many equal parts the whole is divided into. 1/2 means half of the whole.

## Chunk: maths_c3_shapes
Title: Shapes and Geometry
Subject: Maths
Topic: Shapes
Grade: Class 3
Keywords: square, rectangle, triangle, circle, sides, corners, edges
Content:
Shapes are all around us! A square has 4 equal straight sides and 4 corners. A rectangle has 4 sides where opposite sides are equal. A triangle has 3 sides and 3 corners. A circle is round and curved with no straight sides and no corners. In 3D shapes, a cube looks like a dice with 6 flat square faces, and a sphere is shaped like a playing ball.
